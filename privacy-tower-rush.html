1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tower Rush mobile application (the “App”) provided by Big Fish AS (referred to as “Big Fish AS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we or our service providers will request it through an appropriate consent mechanism.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you provide

The App does not require account registration. If you contact us by email or telephone, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and support details.

2.2 Game and app activity

The App may process gameplay and interaction information, such as game sessions, scores, progress, feature usage, button interactions, and basic performance events. Some game progress may be stored locally on your device.

2.3 Device, diagnostics, and technical information

Depending on the device, operating system, settings, and services used in the App, technical information may be processed automatically, including:

  • device type, operating-system version, app version, and language;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • advertising identifiers or similar device identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • crash data, diagnostics, performance information, and error logs;
  • ad impressions, clicks, and interactions with advertising content.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain Tower Rush;
  • save or display game progress and improve gameplay;
  • measure app stability, detect errors, and resolve technical issues;
  • understand how the App is used and improve its design and performance;
  • display, limit, measure, and protect advertising;
  • respond to support, privacy, or legal requests;
  • protect users, the App, and our rights against fraud, misuse, or security threats;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

Tower Rush may use third-party services that process information under their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google Play Services — platform functionality, app distribution, security, and related services;
  • Google AdMob — advertising delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, reporting, and ad measurement.

You can review Google's privacy practices on Google's official privacy-policy pages and manage certain advertising controls through your Google account and device settings.

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party service providers may process data as independent controllers or processors, depending on the service and applicable law.

5. Advertising and consent

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, age settings, device settings, available identifiers, and the consent choices presented to you.

You may be able to reset or delete your device's advertising identifier, limit ad personalization, or change consent choices through your device settings or any consent interface shown in the App.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including support, security, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Retention periods used by Google and other third-party services are governed by their respective policies and configurations.

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is deleted by the App, removed through device settings, or erased when the App is uninstalled, depending on the operating system and backup settings.

7. International processing

Information may be processed in Norway and in other countries where our service providers operate. These countries may have data-protection rules different from those in your country. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To submit a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. In Norway, the supervisory authority is Datatilsynet.

9. Children's privacy

Tower Rush is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the consent required by applicable law. Where a higher minimum age applies in a user's country, that higher age applies.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete the information.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. External links

The App or advertisements shown in the App may link to third-party websites or applications. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of third parties. Review their policies before providing information.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Tower Rush, service providers, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
